Cold coffee Home


Description

Cold coffee is a versatile, chilled coffee beverage enjoyed in many forms, including iced coffee, cold brew, and blended coffee shakes. It can be easily prepared at home using instant coffee, brewed coffee, or espresso, mixed with cold milk, water, sugar, and ice.

Ingridents

  • 1 Tbsp instant coffee powder or granules
  • ¼ cup warm water
  • 2-4 Tbsp sugar (adjust to taste)
  • 2 cups cold or chilled milk (whole milk works best for creaminess)
  • 6-8 ice cubes

Steps

  1. Dissolve the coffee base: In a small bowl or the base of your blender, combine the instant coffee, sugar, and warm water. Stir well until both the coffee and sugar are completely dissolved. This step helps enhance the coffee's flavor and prevents a grainy texture.
  2. Blend to froth: Add the dissolved coffee mixture, the chilled milk, and the ice cubes to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth: Blend on high for a minute or two until the mixture is smooth and a nice frothy layer forms on top. For a thicker, creamier version, you can swap some ice cubes for a scoop of vanilla ice cream before blending.
  4. Serve: Pour the cold coffee into tall glasses and serve immediately, as the frothy layer will settle over time.
