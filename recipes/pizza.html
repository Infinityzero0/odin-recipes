Pizza (Home)

Description

Pizza is an Italian dish typically consisting of a flat base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomato, cheese, and other ingredients, baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven.

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Cheese
  • Souce

Steps

  1. Prepare the Pizza Dough
    • Activate Yeast: Combine lukewarm water (about 110°F), a teaspoon of sugar, and active dry yeast. Let it sit for 5–10 minutes until it becomes foamy.
    • Mix Ingredients: In a large bowl, mix flour (all-purpose or bread flour), salt, and olive oil with the yeast mixture.
    • Knead: Knead the dough by hand or with a stand mixer for 5–10 minutes until it is smooth and elastic.
    • Proof: Place the dough in a greased bowl, cover with a damp cloth or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for 1–2 hours, or until doubled in size
  2. Shape and Prep
    • Preheat Oven: Set your oven to its highest setting, typically between 450°F and 550°F (230°C–290°C). If using a pizza stone, preheat it inside for at least 30–60 minutes.
    • Punch and Divide: Gently deflate the risen dough and divide it into portions for individual pizzas.
    • Stretch the Dough: On a floured surface, stretch or roll the dough into a 10–14 inch circle. Aim for a thin center and a slightly thicker rim to create a crust.
    • Dock the Dough: Prick the center with a fork to prevent large air bubbles from forming during the bake.
  3. Assemble and Bake
    • Add Sauce: Spread a thin layer of pizza sauce over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border for the crust.
    • Layer Toppings: Sprinkle mozzarella cheese evenly, followed by your favorite toppings like pepperoni, vegetables, or fresh herbs.
    • Bake: Slide the pizza onto the preheated stone or a baking sheet. Bake for 8–15 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly.
    • Rest and Serve: Let the pizza rest for 2–5 minutes before slicing to allow the cheese to set.
